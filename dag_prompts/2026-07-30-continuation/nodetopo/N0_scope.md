> Load `skills/seeking-alpha/SKILL.md`, `DAG.md`, NODETOPO terminal reports, campaign plan, and LIVE_STATE.

## Objective / gate

Is there a new named event-local topology mechanism whose required information was not
in the terminal raw-node universe or the 50 restoration actions?

## Tasks

1. State the terminal exclusions precisely: raw-universe construction, existing 50
   actions, and their graph result cannot be rerun or reframed.
2. Generate 3 candidates using representation change plus an independent technique.
3. For each, identify changed evidence, a local synthetic observable, and how it may
   improve association without assuming a graph-score result.

## Output

Write `runs/N0_SCOPE.md` with a proposed (not written) LEDGER row.

## Verdict

- **GO**: one candidate requires information outside the terminal universe and is testable.
- **PARK**: candidates are new sounding but lack an observable.
- **DEAD**: all candidates reconstruct raw-universe/restoration work.
- **ESCALATE**: a protected scorer or terminal action is required.

## Cascade

- On **GO** → proceed directly to `N1_literature.md` in the same run.
- On PARK / DEAD / ESCALATE / HUMAN-DECISION → halt and report; do not advance.
