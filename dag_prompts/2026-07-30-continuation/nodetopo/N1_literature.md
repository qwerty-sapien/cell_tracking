# NODETOPO N1 — local topology evidence audit

> Load `skills/seeking-alpha/SKILL.md`, `DAG.md`, `runs/N0_SCOPE.md`, and LIVE_STATE.

## Objective / gate

Do two independent primary sources support the new local topology observable for
time-lapse cell association, or support a structural transfer with stated assumptions?

## Tasks

1. Collect primary literature with exact supported claims and negative evidence.
2. Map each assumption to information available under the new mechanism only.
3. Reject candidates based solely on generic graph repair or an already terminal node
   restoration concept.

## Output

Write `runs/N1_LITERATURE.md` with citations and a proposed (not written) LEDGER row.

## Verdict

- **GO**: two primary sources plus an explicit local-to-association causal mapping pass.
- **PARK**: evidence lacks an independent source or relevant assumptions.
- **DEAD**: sources contradict the local signal's discriminative value.
- **ESCALATE**: evidence requires forbidden raw-universe reprocessing.

## Cascade

- On **GO** → proceed directly to `N2_falsifier.md` in the same run.
- On PARK / DEAD / ESCALATE / HUMAN-DECISION → halt and report; do not advance.
