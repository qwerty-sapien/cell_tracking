# Division N0 — distinct mechanism gate

> Load `skills/seeking-alpha/SKILL.md`, `DAG.md`, all division terminal reports, the
> campaign plan, and LIVE_STATE.

## Objective / gate

Can a proposed division mechanism explicitly depend on **new representation evidence**,
rather than current-pool selection, an oracle label, D1 refit, or a locked continuity arm?

## Tasks

1. Translate the 37/38 representation-conditioned ceiling into required observable
   information; list 3 candidates from two novelty generators.
2. Reject candidates that amount to a threshold, current-pool selector, fitting, or
   reuse of an unqualified association-continuity arm.
3. State the candidate's new information and its smallest non-fitting observable.

## Output

Write `runs/N0_SCOPE.md` with a proposed (not written) LEDGER row.

## Verdict

- **GO**: ≥1 candidate needs genuinely new representation evidence and has an observable.
- **PARK**: candidates exist but collapse to current-pool selection.
- **DEAD**: all candidates repeat locked/terminal division work.
- **ESCALATE**: the candidate requires a plan change before it can be defined.

## Cascade

- On **GO** → proceed directly to `N1_literature.md` in the same run.
- On PARK / DEAD / ESCALATE / HUMAN-DECISION → halt and report; do not advance.
