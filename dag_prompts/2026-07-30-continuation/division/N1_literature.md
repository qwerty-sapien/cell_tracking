## Objective / gate

Do two independent primary sources support the candidate's use of new representation
information for mitosis/division resolution, with assumptions compatible with this task?

## Tasks

1. Capture primary-source links, exact findings, data assumptions, and counterevidence.
2. Separate evidence for division detection from evidence for lineage association.
3. Specify why the candidate could recover the missing division evidence rather than
   merely reproduce the current-pool oracle.

## Output

Write `runs/N1_LITERATURE.md` with citations and a proposed (not written) LEDGER row.

## Verdict

- **GO**: two primary sources and a representation-specific causal claim pass.
- **PARK**: evidence is plausible but lacks independent support.
- **DEAD**: literature does not support division-relevant information gain.
- **ESCALATE**: a forbidden fitting/held-out action is needed to assess it.

## Cascade

- On **GO** → proceed directly to `N2_falsifier.md` in the same run.
- On PARK / DEAD / ESCALATE / HUMAN-DECISION → halt and report; do not advance.
