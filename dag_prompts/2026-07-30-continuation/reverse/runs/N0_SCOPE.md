The selected move is defensible rather than a generic bidirectional tracker because
it transfers a *cell-contour mechanical constraint* to a graph edge diagnostic:
the individual observable is a mask-boundary round trip plus normal direction, not
two endpoint scores made to agree. It also differs from the campaign's DEAD
geometry/NCC/constant-velocity abstention class: the proposed source uses learned
cross-attention contour correspondence and a cycle-return witness, not a scalar
geometric heuristic.

## Test sketch and crowding check

Before any source/model acquisition, an analytic circle-expansion fixture will
compare a true radial-contour continuation, a zero-motion shortcut, and a wrong
contour. It will require the true continuation to have strictly lower landing plus
return residual, and will kill the candidate if zero flow is accepted. The test is
specified in N2; no fixture asset or campaign data was created here.

Most teams would try a reverse pass or an appearance encoder. The less-obvious
mechanism here is treating the *returned contour field and normal constraint* as
separate causal/persistence evidence for a node association, with a deliberately
anti-trivial null. Its relevance is literature-grounded but unproven on this
campaign; that uncertainty is preserved for N1/N2.
