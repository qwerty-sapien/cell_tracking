# Reverse N1 — future-evidence literature audit

> Load `skills/seeking-alpha/SKILL.md`, `DAG.md`, `runs/N0_SCOPE.md`, and LIVE_STATE.

## Objective / gate

Do two independent primary sources support the candidate's future/causal evidence
observable for noisy tracking or a structurally justified analogue?

## Tasks

1. Capture source links, exact results, model/data assumptions, and counterevidence.
2. Explain why information is independent of endpoint-return Trackastra evidence.
3. Reject generic bidirectional-tracking citations that do not demonstrate the candidate's
   distinct observable or causal mechanism.

## Output

Write `runs/N1_LITERATURE.md` with citations and a proposed (not written) LEDGER row.

## Verdict

- **GO**: two primary sources support a distinct future-evidence mechanism.
- **PARK**: grounding is incomplete or source independence is unresolved.
- **DEAD**: literature undermines the claimed information gain.
- **ESCALATE**: proving independence requires forbidden supplier reuse.

## Cascade

- On **GO** → proceed directly to `N2_falsifier.md` in the same run.
- On PARK / DEAD / ESCALATE / HUMAN-DECISION → halt and report; do not advance.
