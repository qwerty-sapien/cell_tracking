# SA-REPR Phase-1 supplier DAG

Status: preregistered supplier-only lane. No graph, CV57, swap integration, external
download, GPU launch, pseudo-mask, or submission is authorized.

```text
N0 frozen-input hash authentication
  -> N1 local weight/source/license inventory
  -> N2 authentic-mask/wrfeat identity inventory
  -> N3 offline support + deterministic control witnesses
       |-- no authenticated supplier -> PARK local acquisition; preserve families
       `-- one authenticated supplier -> freeze at most one contract/cache
                                          -> root Phase-2 barrier
```

## N0 — frozen inputs

All N1, folds, P0, fit-provenance, locked-plan, prior-H3, and prior-F0 inputs must
match `PREREGISTRATION_v1.json` exactly. Drift fails closed before candidate
outcomes are opened.

## N1 — local weight provenance

Inventory local model artifacts under the three preregistered roots. A filename or
tensor checkpoint is not an authenticated supplier. GO requires all of:

- immutable weight SHA-256;
- exact architecture/interface;
- resolvable local source revision or source-tree identity;
- readable code and weight license evidence;
- offline loader/support proof with no network fallback;
- frozen weights and a point-compatible image interface.

Trackastra association payloads, organizer/detector checkpoints, and competition-fit
compact heads are classified but cannot masquerade as a new dense image supplier.
The DEAD zero-wrfeat mechanism is never rerun.

## N2 — authentic H3 morphology

Audit only the fit105 raw-directory manifest, local source archive inventory, and
frozen sparse-cache paths. Raw intensity chunks and point coordinates are not
instance masks. GO requires complete exact `(identity, frame, node_id)` joins,
origin, license, and hashes. Pseudo-masks and detector reruns are out of scope.

## N3 — support and controls

Before any feature cache, validate a canonical synthetic supplier boundary:

- missing/no-feature returns the baseline bytes exactly;
- repeated within-frame XZ/YZ permutations are byte-identical;
- permutation preserves row identities and XY;
- supported multi-row groups change XZ/YZ;
- unsupported or nonfinite supplier outputs fail closed.

This is a mechanical contract witness, not evidence of graph improvement.

## Terminal cascade

- **GO:** freeze at most one highest-priority authenticated supplier contract/cache
  inside this subtree, self-manifest it, and return it to the root barrier. It must
  not be consumed by SA-SWAP during Phase 1.
- **PARK:** bank the local-acquisition negative while leaving the broader R2/R4/R7
  mechanisms PARK-B. An owner-supplied, provenance-complete artifact may resume them.
- **GPU request:** only after local authentication and a credible projection. Root
  alone may authorize. RunPod is forbidden.

The C2/Branch-B memo numbers are quarantined and play no role.
