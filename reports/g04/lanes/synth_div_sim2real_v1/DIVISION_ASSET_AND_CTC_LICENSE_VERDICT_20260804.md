# Division asset and CTC license verdict — 2026-08-04

## Banked scientific asset

**Daughter assignment is solved conditionally; event detection is not.** On the
sealed real deployment re-analysis, the conditional pair head achieves AUROC
`0.897081275258` and AP `0.368519833394` across 2,292 pairs / 65 positives.
That head is a working banked asset for choosing the two daughters once a true,
reachable dividing parent is supplied.

The separate parent-event head is random: AUROC `0.521520816245`, AP
`3.13665865477e-5` across 2,858,003 parents / 82 positives. The fitted contract
used geometry, lineage, and motion and excluded raw texture/appearance. The
correct disposition is therefore:

> Division event detection has no working mechanism; conditional daughter
> assignment is solved.

This is not “division is unreachable.” Of 83 labelled divisions, 65 reach the
conditional pair population. The 18 outside it are exactly 11 absent from the
detection candidate universe, 4 outside nearest-8, and 3 absent from the pair
universe. The synthetic generator has no r10 cutoff.

The precise scientific unblock is an appearance-capable event detector that,
on the 80,013,717-action deployment denominator, can support at least 5 TP and
no more than 17 FP in the first 22 (precision@22 at least `0.227273`) and an AP
lift of at least `8462x`; the conditional pair head can then be reused without
another daughter-assignment refit. Until this exists, another end-to-end
division attempt is not funded and the `+0.061628263275` ordinary-association
FP surface is the nearer lever.

## Cell Tracking Challenge terms

Authoritative source: the official Cell Tracking Challenge dataset terms,
<https://celltrackingchallenge.net/datasets/> (read 2026-08-04).

The owner’s “citation is sufficient” reading is **not correct for this use**.
The official terms distinguish:

- CTC-related use (preparing a CTC submission, participating in CTC, or
  reporting CTC results), which does not require explicit consent but does
  require the prescribed citation/acknowledgement; and
- public scientific use outside CTC, which requires explicit permission from
  the organizers. Commercial use additionally requires organizer and data-
  provider consent.

Training a detector for the Biohub Kaggle competition is public scientific use
outside CTC. Citation alone therefore does not authorize it. The prior
permission-restricted registration was correct; no CTC archive may be used for
training under the current evidence.

CTC’s real annotated divisions are nevertheless the leading **permission-
dependent** candidate for an appearance-based event detector. Record them as
`PARK-A-PERMISSION`: written organizer permission is the dependency. If that
permission is obtained with terms covering this competition, the dataset can
open an appearance-event node; without it, CTC remains excluded. This verdict
does not close division by default and does not authorize a fifth attempt.
